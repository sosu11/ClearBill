# ClearBill

Medical bills are hard to read. ClearBill takes a photo or PDF of one and explains it in plain English: what each charge is, which ones are worth asking the provider about, and why.

**Try it:** https://sosu11.github.io/ClearBill/

## How it works

1. Upload a photo or PDF of a medical bill, or try the built-in sample bill.
2. ClearBill reads every line item and explains it in simple language.
3. It flags charges that look unusual, like duplicates, vague "miscellaneous" lines, or totals that don't add up, and suggests what to ask billing.
4. Answer a few quick questions about your visit and the flags update.

Flags are "worth asking about," not accusations. ClearBill can't see your medical records or insurance contract, so it only points out questions worth raising.

## Privacy

- ClearBill has no server and no accounts. Nothing is stored beyond your open browser tab, and "Delete everything" clears it at any time.
- Bills go from your browser straight to Google's Gemini API, using your own free [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey) key. On Google's free tier, Google may use submitted content to improve its products, so use the sample bill, or cover your name and account numbers, if you'd rather not share them.

This tool provides information, not medical, legal, or financial advice. Verify anything important with your provider or insurer.
